Plant Guide

Plant Guide is a Linked Data website based around the theme of plant photography, specifically the works done by Photographer William Eggleston. William Eggleston is an American photographer who is known for his unqiue approach to capturing everyday scenes. He often focuses on the overlooked aspects of life. His photographs are characterized by their vibrant colors, meticulous composition, and attention to detail.

Plant Guide aims to capture the handmade, and mundane feeling of his photography and highlight it through its own handmade feel.
